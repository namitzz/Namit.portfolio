Privacy and cookies.

Last updated 14 September 2026

  • No cookies. Nothing on this site sets one.
  • No advertising and no tracking across other websites.
  • No forms. The site never asks you for personal details.
  • Visits are counted with cookieless analytics that cannot identify you.

Who is responsible

This is the personal portfolio of Namit Singh Sarna, who is responsible for the personal data described on this page. For anything about your data, email namitmec@gmail.com.

What is collected, and why

Visitor statistics. The site uses Vercel Web Analytics to see how many people visit and which parts they read. For each page view it records the page, the site that referred you, your approximate location (country, region and city), your browser, operating system and device type, and the time. It uses no cookies. Vercel tells a visit apart with a hash made from the request, and discards it after 24 hours, so the statistics are aggregated and cannot be tied back to you. The lawful basis is legitimate interests: understanding whether the portfolio is being read. Details are in Vercel's analytics privacy documentation.

Hosting. The site is hosted by Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel processes your IP address and basic request details in order to deliver pages and keep the service secure, as described in Vercel's privacy notice.

If you email me. I receive your email address and whatever you choose to write, and use them only to reply to you. They are not added to a mailing list or shared with anyone.

What is stored on your device

No cookies, and nothing in local storage. There is one item in session storage:

Name Purpose Lasts
namitss_intro_played_v1 Remembers that the opening animation has played, so it does not replay every time you reload. It holds no information about you and is not used for analytics. Until you close the tab

Third parties

Fonts are served from this site rather than from Google, so loading a page does not contact Google. Nothing is embedded from other services. Links to GitHub, LinkedIn and the project sites take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to access, correct or delete personal data held about you, or object to its use. The analytics are anonymous, so there is usually nothing in them that can be traced to you; for anything else, email the address above. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Changes

If what the site collects changes, this page will change with it, and the date at the top will say when.
